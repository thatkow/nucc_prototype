About us

We explore responsibly

NUCC leads students underground, underwater, and across mountain plateaus with a focus on safety, stewardship, and community. Our leaders are trained in vertical systems, wilderness medicine, and environmental protection.

Mentored training

Structured progression from intro nights to advanced canyoning and expedition leadership.

Inclusive community

Everyone is welcome. We provide loaner gear, sliding-scale fees, and quiet social spaces.

Conservation first

We partner with cave scientists to monitor habitats and reduce human impact.
