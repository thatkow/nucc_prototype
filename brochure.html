Trips & Training

Regular caving and canyoning trips — most beginner friendly.

Weekly Single Rope Technique (SRT) training to master vertical caves.

Gear & Support

No experience or equipment required — we provide helmets, headtorches, and more.

Shared equipment maintenance ($5 when using club gear) keeps the gear library running.

Expeditions

Overseas and interstate expeditions — recent destinations include New Zealand and Tasmania.

Access to permit-only caves the public will never see.

From the Archives

Jenolan-Kempsey Big Trip

Another one of NUCC's big end of year trips. This time, it saw us going to Jenolan to meet up with SUSS for a couple of days of caving in Barralong (helping dive trips), Wiburds and Mammoth (survey work and fun); then heading up north to Kempsey, where we checked out caves in and around the Kookaburra and Moparabah areas.

Get Started

How to Join

Navigate to NUCC on UniOne, ANU Sport’s club membership website. Create an account if you do not already have one, then select the appropriate membership option.

Fill out all online forms and pay through UniOne. You’re ready to go! Membership lasts for one rolling year.

ANU students: 2023 Caving Student and Life Members Membership

Non-students and non-ANU students: 2023 Caving Associate Membership

Once your UniOne membership payment is confirmed, request access to our private Facebook group to hear about trip sign-ups and organise car pooling.

Membership must be paid before we can send you a group invitation.
