Reference Figures

Impact in Motion

All figures quoted directly from NUCC membership information and archived trip notes.

Trip Rhythm

Fortnightly

Our trips run roughly every fortnight during the university semester and welcome all levels of experience.

Day Trips

$10–$20

Usually this adds up to $10–$20 for a day trip, depending on location and shared costs.

Weekend Trips

$25–$50

Weekend and longer trips often include camping or hut stays and typically cost $25–$50.

Membership

Annual Fees

  • $55 per year for ANU students
  • $75 + $120 club affiliation fee for non-students

Yearly fees include ANU Sport insurance and gear maintenance.

Trip Components

Shared Expenses

  • Shared equipment maintenance ($5 when using club gear)
  • Personal gear hire, including an SRT kit if needed ($5)
  • Shared fuel costs plus any camping or park access fees

Community Logistics

Car Pooling

We car pool to and from caving sites departing from ANU Sport, so you do not need a car to join. Fuel costs are shared between trip attendees.

Club Access

Gear Library

No experience or equipment required — we provide helmets, headtorches, and more.

Once your UniOne membership payment is confirmed, request access to our private Facebook group to hear about trip sign-ups and organise car pooling.
