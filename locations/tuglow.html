Tuglow Caving Adventure

Exploring the underground world beneath the limestone plains of Tuglow

1. The Drive and Hike In

Leaving the main road, we wound through ridgelines and valleys, the bush thickening as the track narrowed. The final stretch was on foot — packs heavy, the forest silent except for the crunch of gravel and the rustle of wind. Tuglow’s limestone cliffs appeared suddenly, marking the hidden entrance to the caves below.

2. Rigging the Entrance for SRT

At the mouth of the cave, the air turned cool and damp. We rigged ropes from sturdy limestone ledges, double-checking anchors before the first descent. The distant echo of water hinted at the vast network below as we clipped in and descended into darkness.

3. The Underground Creek

Deep below, a trickle turned into a stream that guided our path. We followed the underground creek through twisting chambers, climbing over slick boulders and crawling beneath low ceilings. Each passage led us further into Tuglow’s hidden heart.

4. The Underground Lake

The passage opened into a vast chamber where an underground lake shimmered under our lights. The surface was mirror-still, reflecting the jagged ceiling like glass. We paused here, switching off our lamps — total darkness, total silence — a world untouched.

5. The Tall Aven

Further along, a tall aven stretched upward into the unseen. Its walls were carved by centuries of dripping water, streaked with minerals and glistening under our headlamps. A reminder that even in the depths, the world above still leaves its mark.

6. Resurfacing and Heading Home

Climbing back up the entrance pitch, the first glimmer of daylight felt unreal. Emerging into the crisp air, we packed our gear and shared quiet smiles — tired, muddy, and exhilarated. Tuglow had revealed just a glimpse of its underground wonder.
