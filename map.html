How to explore

  • 1Zoom and pan around the map to find the trip areas we've spotlighted.
  • 2Click a marker to open a rich tooltip with a short trip highlight and photo gallery.
  • 3Use the reset view button if you lose your bearings.

About this prototype

The map uses OpenStreetMap tiles via Leaflet and a small curated set of locations drawn from recent NUCC adventures. It is a jumping-off point for a richer trip archive.

Next ideas
