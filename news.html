From the Archive

Jenolan-Kempsey Big Trip · 2003

Another one of NUCC's big end of year trips. This time, it saw us going to Jenolan to meet up with SUSS for a couple of days of caving in Barralong (helping dive trips), Wiburds and Mammoth (survey work and fun); then heading up north to Kempsey, where we checked out caves in and around the Kookaburra and Moparabah areas.

Day 1 (Canberra – Jenolan) Sunday 30/11: After getting together at Olaf’s place and having a late lunch, left Canberra about 2:30pm to reach Jenolan by sunset. Made it to Caver’s Cottage and the SUSSlings.

Trip Cost Reminder

Costs are kept as low as possible — we are mostly poor, broke uni students after all! Expect up to four components.

  • Shared equipment maintenance ($5 when using club gear)
  • Personal gear hire, including an SRT kit if needed ($5)
  • Shared fuel costs
  • Camping or accommodation fees, plus park access if applicable

Usually this adds up to $10–$20 for a day trip, and $25–$50 for an overnight or longer trip.
