Timeline of Discovery

Trace the underground journeys that shaped the National University Caving Club.

2025

River Rift Breakthrough

Teams charted 600m of new passage using DistoX mapping and drone recon. The discovery introduced new freshwater pools that will be monitored by the conservation group.

Highlights: subterranean river, quartz formations, trip leader notes.

Swift-water rigging drills and canyon mapping with GPS overlays.

2024

Cascade Canyon Reopens

After restoration work, we re-rigged the canyon with eco-friendly anchors. Over 40 members participated in the reopening weekend.

2023

Vertical Rescue Certification

NUCC earned national recognition after certifying 20 leaders in advanced cave rescue through a week-long scenario course.

Scenario focus: stretcher hauls, communication relays, medical response.

Multi-day expedition with underground camps, survey data, and ecological sampling.

2022

First International Expedition

NUCC partnered with European clubs to explore karst systems in Slovenia, exchanging rigging techniques and conservation practices.

2021

Guidebook Launch

Our first digital knowledge base went live, featuring safety protocols and training modules.

Modules include trip planning, risk assessment, and conservation ethics.
